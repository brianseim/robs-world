**Feats:**
- **Scribe Scroll** – Create scrolls (PH pg 99)
- **Spell Mastery** – Prepare up to 5 spells without spellbook (PH pg 100). Spells marked with \* in [[Spellbook]]
- **Parry** – Deflect melee attacks (Rob's Snowflake Feats pg 4)
- **Toughness** – +3 HP (PH pg 101) — already included in HP total
- **Simple Weapon Proficiency (Quarterstaff)** – Class/Feat (PH pg 100)
- **Alertness** *(from familiar [[Labraen]])* – +2 Spot and Listen while Labraen is within arm's reach (PH pg 83)
- **Spell Focus (Illusion)** – +1 to save DCs for illusion spells (PH pgs 53 & 99)

**Racial Abilities (Moon Elf):**
- Immune to magical sleep effects (PH pg 16)
- +2 save vs. enchantment spells/effects (PH pg 15)
- Low-light vision (PH pg 16)
- +2 to Listen, Search, and Spot checks (PH pg 16)

**Class Abilities:**
- Summon Familiar (PH pg 57) → [[Labraen]]
